# Certified grouping, projections and continuation

The constructor starts from local operations admitted in a constituted context. It produces a terminal target, its trace and its certificate. The common target of compatible groupings is derived from local joins and strict decrease. In the relational instance, the constructor recovers the existing selection and its exact width for every finite role history.

French version: [Regroupements certifiés, projections et continuation](regroupements-certifies.fr.md). [V5 reproduction](validation-v5.en.md) specifies the technical scope and execution evidence.

## 1. Rule class and generic result

Declarations in this section belong to `RelationalFoundations.CertifiedGrouping`.

`Rules` receives only these local data:

| Field | Local data or law |
|---|---|
| `State` | Configurations in the context under consideration. |
| `Step x y` | A witness in `Type` of an admitted effective operation from `x` to `y`, retaining its operational identity. |
| `rank` | A natural measure of a configuration. |
| `choices x` | A finite list of available target/witness pairs. `Step` carries their admission. |
| `locate` | For every admitted operation, a positive member of that list and heterogeneous agreement on the complete pair. |
| `decreases` | Every effective operation strictly decreases the measure. |
| `diamond` | For two operations from the same source, a constructed target and two directed traces to it. |

`Step` defines admission before normalization. The choice lists are exact for that admission. The terminal target, global frontier and complete solution are subsequent outputs.

`Trace` retains successive witnesses and endpoints. Its empty trace realizes the neutral action. `Trace.append`, `append_assoc`, `act_append` and `act_preserves` construct and certify composition. `Join` contains a common target and two directed traces. `Chain` allows links in either direction; its operational realization uses the directed traces of `joinOfChain`.

`Rules.normalizeWithin` recurses structurally on a counter sufficient for the measure. `normalize` initializes it with `rank x` and returns `Normalized`: target, trace and terminality. `terminal_of_empty` uses `locate` to prove terminality when the choice list is empty. `terminal_unique` derives terminal uniqueness from `diamond` and `decreases`.

Writing `N = Rules.normal`, the conclusions are:

\[
N(N(x))=N(x),\qquad
N(x)=N(y)\iff \mathrm{Nonempty}(\mathrm{Join}(x,y))
\iff \mathrm{Nonempty}(\mathrm{Chain}(x,y)).
\]

`joinOfNormalEq` and `joinOfChain` construct join witnesses in `Type`. `confluence` handles any two traces from one source. `trace_length_le` bounds every effective trace by the initial measure. Termination concerns grouping under the rules of the examined context; continuation may constitute another context with further operations.

## 2. Joint readings and composed identifications

For projections `P i`, `ProjectionFamily.JointAgreement P x y` states equality under **all** readings. `ProjectionFamily.Path P x y` retains a chain whose individual links choose **one** projection and its equality. These relations retain their respective quantifiers.

`Path.minimal` proves the closure property generated by these links. `Path.invariant` propagates any reading respecting every local identification. For a connected family, `boundary_constant` proves that a Boolean boundary respecting all those links is constant on every pair of sources.

`ProjectionFamily.Authorized` receives a constructed admitted join for each local equality. `realizePath` produces the join for every chain. Given `stepIdentification`, positively associating an identifying projection with each operation, `exact_fibres` characterizes the fibres of `N` exactly by these chains.

`BinaryGrouping` closes this contract on profiles of binary positions. A local projection selects its authorized position and retains the others. With at least two positions, `joint_injective` proves that joint readings distinguish sources. When all positions are authorized, `fullyConnected` proves that composed identifications connect all sources. `projections_each_separate` and `nevertheless_connected`, in `Tests/CertifiedGrouping.lean`, provide the four-profile example: two sources have distinct readings under each projection while belonging to the same generated component.

## 3. Obligations and exact frontiers

`FiniteImage.Target` is the type of targets certified stable under `N`. `carry` produces `N(x)` with its stability proof. `carry_surjective` uses a stable target as its positive source. `carry_fibres` retains exact agreement with admitted chains.

`FiniteImage.Source` explicitly adds decidable equality, a complete finite enumeration and absence of duplicates. `frontier` normalizes that enumeration and removes repetitions. `frontier_exact` proves that its members are exactly the stable targets. Transported data remain arbitrary; this equality decision compares configuration identities within the declared domain.

`FiniteImage.Composed` declares a produced frontier with exactness and duplicate-free certificates. The generic interface receives that construction; the relational adapter closes it using existing fragment producers. `exhaustive_composed` proves membership agreement between both frontiers; `composed_width` proves equal width. `singleton_of_connected` requires a distinguished source and connectedness; `empty_image` handles the empty domain.

Trace length measures grouping operations. Width measures produced obligations. The exhaustive image enumerates the source domain; the relational fragment construction uses `rolewiseObligationFrontier`. Width agreements retain this difference between producers. Results bound effective steps; the programs expose the separate costs of choice procedures and image composition.

## 4. Relational history instance

Adapters live in `Migration/RelationalPerimeter/Computation/ConstitutiveSearch/EndogenousDecomposition/`. Their namespace is `ConstitutiveSearch.EndogenousDecomposition.CertifiedRoleGrouping`.

`roleShape`, `encode`, `decode` and `code` establish a reversible correspondence with native profiles. Reconstruction retains the actually executed role and its constituted occurrence. `GroupingReindex` transports binary rules to that domain, with exact agreement on target/witness pairs. The provided reindexer starts from rules with carriers in `Type 0`; the reindexed domain may inhabit a higher universe.

For every `RoleStatus.History`:

- a pending status retains both occurrences;
- a present transport authorizes the directed action from left to right;
- local choices and joins are constructed from the history structure;
- the measure counts authorized positions still to transform in the source profile.

`normal_selected` proves that the constructed normalizer recovers `History.selected`. Existing selection appears as an agreement conclusion. `targetTransport` supplies a reversible correspondence between existing obligations and generic stable targets. `carry_fibres`, `projection_fibres` and `targetTransport_carry` retain carrying maps and their identities. `composed_width` and `exhaustive_width` give:

\[
\mathrm{width}=2^{\mathrm{History.pendingCount}}.
\]

`pendingCount` counts positions retaining two obligations; the normalization measure counts a profile's remaining actions. All mixed statuses are covered with their supplied transports.

Concrete closure uses `RoleStatus.executed reduction`, whose transports come from `returnedTransport`. `stagewise_normal` uses `ofStagewise_eq_executed` to identify produced licences. `executed_normal`, `executed_join`, `executed_path` and `executed_boundary` connect profiles to the realization's retained profile. The executed frontier has one obligation. The earlier characterization of full width by injective separate preservation remains exposed through `ConstitutiveExtensiveSeparation` and the migrated public entry point.

## 5. Data, transports and continuation

`Normative` supplies indexed data, an acceptance criterion and each operation's certified action. `transport_preserves` preserves acceptance on every trace for every accepted datum; `transport_composes` proves pointwise agreement under composition.

In the relational instance, `codedAction` and `act` use licence-associated transports. `all_trace_acceptance` covers every history, trace and accepted datum. `completed_trace`, in `RoleGroupingSemantics.lean`, additionally preserves the completed transformation, deriving:

| Declaration | Quantifiers and agreement |
|---|---|
| `every_normalizing_trace` | Every history, profile, trace to its selection and datum: transport equals `History.transform`. |
| `normalization_coherent` | Two traces to that selection give equal transports on each datum. |
| `executed_trace_output` | Every normalization trace in an executed realization: canonical data yield the actually retained operational target. |

These agreements are derived in the instance. The generic normalization contract concerns configurations; `Normative.Coherent` separately expresses optional transport coherence on all endpoint pairs. `equal_target_different_transport` witnesses the need for this additional law.

`Continuation.Exact` receives two total steps, their readings, events and admissions in `Type`. It requires local step agreement and both admission transports. `run_exact`, `events_exact`, `observations_exact`, `admitted` and `admittedSource` propagate agreement over every finite input list. This interface observes the state before each step. `stable` proves projected successor stability; `run_append` and `events_append` close suffix composition.

`Native.bridge`, in `GroupingInstances.lean`, closes this contract using `forget`, `step`, `forget_step` and `forget_matches`. Its source retains historical execution as a correspondence witness. Separate reduced producers `reducedNext`, `reducedEvent` and `reducedRead` use only native memory, consisting of `position` and `active`, and the deterministic step's unit input. `all_finite` covers every list of those inputs. `all_native_admissible` covers every suffix of the independent native predicate `HeterogeneousFeedback.Admissible`, from every `Execution` witness supplied as a source. Currently this admission determines its suffix from the source and its length.

`all_new_certifications` closes new evaluations in the actually extended support; `references_compose` preserves typed references across two continuations. Existing `FiniteRuleCoordinates` theorems preserve coordinates, operators and ordered dependencies in both homogeneous and heterogeneous realizations, including resumptions. `forgotten_decision` reuses the positive separator of equal memories and different decisions to exclude a uniform decoder.

## 6. Historical extensions

`Extension` receives an inclusion and a transport of each old operation into an admitted trace in the next context. Further authorizations may enable further grouping. `renormalized` proves:

\[
N_{h'}(\iota(N_h(x)))=N_{h'}(\iota(x)).
\]

`obligation` normalizes the transported target in that new context. `obligation_composes` and `obligation_identity` prove composition and identity agreement for obligations.

`Historical`, in `HistoricalRoleGrouping.lean`, retains identical previously executed roles. Its `suffix` case receives a profile of new occurrences; `same` preserves an authorization; `license` adds a transport at a previously pending position; `composed` retains both constructed extensions. `embedding_injective` preserves source identities, `liftMove` transports operations, and `extension` closes the generic contract. `renormalized`, `embedding_composes` and `obligation_composes` give native agreements. `executedSuffix` supplies the new profile from the retained realization; `returnedGrowth` closes new authorization using the actually returned licence.
