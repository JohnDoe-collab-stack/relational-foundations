# Conceptual authorship and AI-generation disclosure

## English

The project owner declares that the essential ideas, originating research
questions, central conceptual distinctions, and research direction of this
project are their own.

The project owner also declares that the entire textual and code contents of
this repository were written from start to finish by models in OpenAI's ChatGPT
model series over successive interactions and under human direction. This
includes the Lean source code, formal statements, proofs, examples,
documentation, translations, metadata, audit materials, scripts, and repository
organization.

The human contribution consisted of originating the essential ideas and
research direction, formulating the objectives, selecting and rejecting
proposals, requiring revisions and rigor, reviewing the results, and making
publication decisions. The models performed the textual, formal, and code
production through which those ideas were formalized, implemented, and
expressed in the repository.

### Development history

This project forms part of a personal research investigation begun around
2020, approximately two years before the public launch of ChatGPT. Since the
public launch of ChatGPT on November 30, 2022, the research has been developed
and formalized through sustained interactions with successive models in
OpenAI's ChatGPT model series, under the project owner's direction.

This timeline describes the history of the research process. It does not claim
that the present repository or each of its files existed throughout that
entire period.

This statement records the declared generation provenance of the artifact. It
does not imply that OpenAI owns, publishes, endorses, or independently certifies
the project. Formal correctness is established separately by the Lean kernel
and by the reproducibility checks included in the repository.

## Français

de la direction de recherche du projet.

Le responsable du projet déclare également que l'intégralité du contenu textuel
et informatique de ce dépôt a été écrite de A à Z par des modèles de la série

Ce projet s'inscrit dans une investigation personnelle commencée vers 2020,

public de ChatGPT, le 30 novembre 2022, la recherche a été développée et
formalisée au cours d'interactions soutenues avec les modèles successifs de la
série ChatGPT d'OpenAI, sous la direction du responsable du projet.

Cette chronologie décrit l'histoire du processus de recherche. Elle ne signifie
pas que le présent dépôt ou chacun de ses fichiers existait pendant toute cette
période.

Cette déclaration consigne la provenance de génération déclarée de l'artefact.
Elle ne signifie pas qu'OpenAI possède, publie, approuve ou certifie
indépendamment le projet. La correction formelle est établie séparément par le
